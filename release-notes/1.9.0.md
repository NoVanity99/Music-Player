## What's new

- **Advanced transitions on certain albums**: on albums made to flow together, each song now runs straight into the next with no gap or crossfade. Albums that have it show a red "Advanced transition" badge beside the album name. Turn it off in Settings if you prefer.
- **Equalizer on Windows**: presets and five bands, under Settings.
- **Discord**: the album cover now comes straight from the music library, so it shows up reliably; the Vanity Player logo is used when a song has no cover.
- **Artist pages**: click, hold and slide a row to move along it.
- **Sharper text**: titles no longer lose the edge of their last letter.
- The last moment of a song is no longer cut short when the next one starts (Windows).
