## What's new

- **Album covers on Discord, from your own files** – your Discord status shows the cover art embedded in the song (or the cover image in its folder), with the Vanity Player logo as a small badge. Songs without a cover show the logo.
- Discord can only show pictures from the internet, so the cover (shrunk to 512 px) is uploaded to Litterbox, a temporary image host that deletes it after 72 hours. Each cover is uploaded once and reused. No searching online.
