## What's new

- **Messages**: chat with your friends. Open the Friends page and tap the message button next to a friend. Unread messages show on the button and in the side menu, and you'll get a notice when a new one arrives.
- **Share music with friends**: pick **Share with a friend** from the menu of any song, album, EP, single or playlist, add a note if you like, and choose who gets it. They can play it or add it to their queue straight from the chat.
- You'll see when a friend has read your messages.
