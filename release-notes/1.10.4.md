## What's new

- **Invite a friend**: the Friends page has an Invite button next to friends who are online. They get a small pop-up and join your session if they accept.
- **Your Library is what you've liked**: Songs shows your liked songs, Albums your hearted albums and Artists your hearted artists. Playlists are unchanged. "Show everything" switches back to the full list.
- **Heart albums**: album pages have a heart next to the title to keep the album in your library.
