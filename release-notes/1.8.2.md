## What's new

- **Artist pages open in one go**: all the artwork is loaded first and the whole page appears together, instead of covers popping in one by one. Covers are now saved to your device ahead of time, so pages open with their art straight away.
- **Smoother**: hovering a card no longer nudges the cards around it, and the artist bio shows immediately once it has been loaded before.
- **Your top**: a new button on artist pages plays the songs by that artist you play the most, and the page shows how often you've played them.
- **New music notice**: when songs are added to the library while the player is open, it tells you what arrived.
- **Disc numbers**: albums with more than one disc now show "Disc 1", "Disc 2" headings.
- A fresher artist page header, and album cards there show the year and song count.
