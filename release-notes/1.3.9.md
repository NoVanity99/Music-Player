## What's new

- **Real albums on artist pages** – under the bio, see the artist's studio albums by year (from MusicBrainz). The ones already in your library are marked and open your copy.
- **Your stats** – how long you've listened, your top artists, songs and albums (7 days, 30 days or all time), your day streak, and your listening personality with a chart of when you listen. Find it in the Home header or the sidebar.
- **Made for you** – daily mixes on Home: *Forgotten favorites*, *Deep cuts*, *Time machine* and *Never played*.
- **Visualizer** (Windows) – animated spectrum bars in the full-screen player, driven by the music actually playing. Turn it off in Settings.
- **Mini player** (Windows) – a small always-on-top player. Press **Ctrl+M** or the new button in the player bar; the expand button brings the full app back.
