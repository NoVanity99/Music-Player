## What's new

- **Vanity Player** – the app has a new name, and the logo in the top left is now animated.
- **Cleaner window on Windows** – minimize, maximize and close are built into the app instead of the standard Windows title bar. Drag the top of the window to move it, double-click it to maximize, and drag any edge to resize.
- **Volume slider in the full-screen player.**

Note for Windows: because the app was renamed, it may not reopen by itself after this update. Start it from the Start menu or desktop shortcut ("Vanity Player").
