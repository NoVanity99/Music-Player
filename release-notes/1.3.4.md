## What's new

- **More reliable Discord Rich Presence** – if Discord doesn't accept the status, the app now retries with a simpler one instead of silently giving up.
- **Discord status in Settings** – under the Discord Rich Presence switch you can see what's happening: connected (and as whom), Discord not running, or the error Discord gave.
- A log of the Discord connection is saved to `%APPDATA%\MusicPlayer\discord.log` to help track down problems.
