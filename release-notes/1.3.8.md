## What's new

- **Click each artist in a collaboration** – in songs credited like "Drake & Future" or "Artist feat. Someone", each name is its own link. Artists who aren't in your library tell you so instead. Band names such as "Mumford & Sons" stay as one artist.
- **Richer artist pages** – besides their own songs, artist pages now show the songs they **appear on** with others, their full **discography** from your library, and an **About** section with a short bio from Wikipedia (turn it off in Settings → Library → Artist bios).
- **Albums stay together** – on Windows, a featured track no longer splits off into its own copy of the album, and albums show their main artist instead of "Various artists".
