## What's new

- **Cloud only** – Vanity Player now plays music only from your cloud library. It no longer looks for music files on your device, and on Android it no longer asks for storage permission.
- The first-run screen asks for your server address and access key instead of a music folder.
- Folder settings, the Folders tab and "Rescan library" are gone. Downloads for offline play work as before.

Note: songs that came from folders on your device disappear from the library, playlists and history after this update. Upload them to your cloud library to get them back.
