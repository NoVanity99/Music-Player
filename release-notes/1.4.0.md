## What's new

- **Cloud library** – stream your own music from your own server (a Cloudflare Worker in front of R2; see `cloud/README.md` in the repository). Enter the server address and access key in Settings → Cloud library. Cloud songs appear in the same library as your local music.
- **Offline downloads** – tap the cloud icon on a song to download it, or use Download on an album, artist or playlist, or "Download all" in Settings. Downloads continue in the background, show progress, and can be cancelled.
- **Offline mode** – without internet, the app shows your local and downloaded songs. There's a new Downloads page with the storage used, and an "Offline" filter on the Songs tab.
- **Windows** – typing in a text field no longer triggers keyboard shortcuts.
