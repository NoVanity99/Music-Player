## What's new

- **Account page**: a new Account entry in the sidebar (and on Home on phones) shows your liked songs, playlists, your artists and your stats in one place.
- **Bio**: add a short bio if you want one.
- **Profiles and privacy**: look up someone by username to see their profile. Your bio, liked songs, playlists and artists can each be set to Public or Private.
- **Your artists**: "See all" on Home now opens just the artists you've hearted, and hearted artists show a heart next to their name in the Artists list and in Search.
