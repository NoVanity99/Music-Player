## What's new

- **Artist pictures**: every artist now gets a real picture, found automatically, for their profile picture and the banner on their page. When none is found, an album cover is used as before.
- **Choose your own**: open the three-dot menu on an artist's page and pick **Choose pictures** to set their profile picture and banner. Search for pictures, or use any of their album covers.
- **Singles for everyone on them**: a single now shows in the Singles row of every artist credited on it.
- **New artist pages**: an artist who only appears alongside someone else on a single now gets a page of their own.
- Artist pictures can be turned off in Settings, under Library.
