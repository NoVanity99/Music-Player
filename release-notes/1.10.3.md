## What's new

- **Admin badge**: the admin's name now carries a gold "ADMIN" bubble with a crown that catches the light. Everyone sees it on their profile and in friends lists.
- **Report a bug**: Settings has a new Help section where you can describe a problem and send it.
- **Tidier Settings**: settings are grouped into sections that open when you click them, so the page is a short list instead of a long scroll.
