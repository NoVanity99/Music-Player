## What's new

- **Friends**: add friends by username. The new Friends page (and your Account page) shows each friend and what they're listening to right now.
- **Listen along**: ask a friend who's playing something to listen along. They get a small pop-up to accept or decline; once accepted you both hear the same song at the same place.
- **In a session**: the host controls playback. A guest can add songs to the host's queue, and "Leave session" takes them back to what they were playing before.
- **Listen privately**: a switch on the Friends page hides what you're playing from friends.
