## What's new

- **New artist pages** – the bio now sits at the top beside Play and Shuffle, followed by four rows: **Custom albums**, **Released albums**, **EPs** and **Singles**, each ordered oldest to newest with the newest on the right. A row with nothing in it says so. All current albums are custom albums.
- **Download liked songs** – a new switch in Settings → Cloud library: liking a song also saves it for offline play.
- **Windows: no more top bar** – minimize, maximize and close now float in the top-right corner inside the app and are always visible. Drag the top of the window to move it; double-click an empty spot there to maximize.
- **Smoother feel** – artwork fades in, lists glide when they change, and sections ease into place.
- The "official albums" list and MusicBrainz are gone.
