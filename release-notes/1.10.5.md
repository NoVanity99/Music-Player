## What's new

- **Full-screen player**: shows the album name; Up next and Lyrics now sit centred under the volume bar, leaving more room for the cover. The pull-down handle is a little lower, and only that top bar drags the player down.
- **New visualizer**: mirrored, glowing bars that flow with the music, with falling peak caps.
- **Mini player**: shows the album name and can be resized from any edge. Shrink it right down and it becomes just the cover.
- **Scrolling text**: titles that don't fit now scroll instead of being cut off.
- **Centred text** under artwork everywhere, including artist pages.
- **Downloads**: a Download button on album, artist, playlist and other pages. On each song, the cloud icon lights up when you point at it; click it to download.
