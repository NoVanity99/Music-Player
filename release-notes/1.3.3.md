## What's new

- **Album covers on Discord** – your Discord status now shows the album cover of the song you're playing, with the Vanity Player logo as a small badge. Covers are looked up online by artist and album name; if none is found, the logo is shown.
- **Android updates fixed** – in-app updates were wrongly rejected with "isn't signed with this app's key". *If you're on 1.3.2 or earlier, install this version by hand once: download the APK below and open it. It installs over your current app and keeps your music and playlists. Updates work from the app after that.*
- **Smoother Windows updates** – the app now reopens by itself after updating, without error messages.
