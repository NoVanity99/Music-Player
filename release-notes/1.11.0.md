## What's new

- **Profile looks**: Settings → Profile lets you style your profile with a live preview: 12 banners, 8 picture frames (two of them moving), 7 name styles (including a gold shine and a moving rainbow) and a flair emoji. Friends and anyone who opens your profile see it.
- **Customize**: a new Settings section to shape the player.
  - Choose the tab it opens on.
  - Switch each Home row on or off: Quick picks, Made for you, Recently played, On repeat, Recently added, Your artists and Your playlists.
  - On Windows, choose what the sidebar holds: playlists, Downloads, Account, Friends, Your stats and Shortcuts.
  - Small details: song length next to the artist, album name in the now-playing bar, centred text under artwork, scrolling titles, and cards reacting to the pointer.
