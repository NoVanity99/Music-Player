## What's new

- **Clicks always register**: a tiny mouse movement during a click was being treated as a drag, so buttons sometimes needed two or three tries. Fixed.
- **Your artists**: heart an artist on their page, and Home's "Your artists" row shows just the ones you've hearted. It follows your account to your other devices.
- **Advanced transitions**: the next song is now opened and its first seconds decoded ahead of time, so it starts the instant the current one ends. Gaia 2 joins the albums that have it.
- **Albums**: click the cover to see it full screen, and the album's year is shown.
- **Songs**: each song's length is shown next to the artist.
- **Now playing bar**: shows the album name as well.
- **Home**: titles are centred under their artwork.
- Folders are gone from Search.
