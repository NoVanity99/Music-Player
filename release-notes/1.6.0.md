## What's new

- **Accounts** – create an account (or sign in) when you open the app. Your liked songs, playlists and listening history belong to your account, so your stats, mixes and "Most played" are yours alone and follow you to any device. Everyone still shares the same music library.
- The first account created on a device keeps the likes, playlists and history that were already there.
- Settings → Account: see who's signed in, sign out, or delete your account.
- **Windows** – no more command window during updates. (You'll still see it one last time while updating to this version.)
